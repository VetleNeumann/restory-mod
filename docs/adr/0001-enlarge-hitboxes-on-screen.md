# Enlarge hitboxes on screen, never resize the game's colliders

Small things at the workbench are hard to hover with a gamepad, so the mod gives them a minimum hitbox size. We measure and enlarge hitboxes on screen (the projected outline of a thing's colliders, grown in pixels) and decide the hovered thing ourselves, instead of resizing the game's colliders. Those colliders do other jobs: a loose part's hitbox is its physics body, a bench object's is also its storage footprint and drag collision, and part ghosts take their size from a part's placement box, so resizing them would change how things drop, collide and fit. A minimum measured in the world would also still look tiny when zoomed out, while an on-screen minimum fades out by itself as the view zooms in. A sphere-cast pick radius was rejected too: it grows every hitbox, large ones included. The hitbox overlay draws the same on-screen shapes the enlarging uses, so the picture and the logic cannot drift apart.
